# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 5, 2026
**Product:** Mosaic for Reddit
**Developer:** MrJohnDowe
**Website:** Mosaic for Reddit

## 1. Overview

Mosaic for Reddit ("Mosaic", "the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") is a browser extension designed to provide an enhanced way to browse media content on Reddit.

Mosaic transforms supported Reddit pages, including subreddits, user profiles, and custom feeds, into a media-focused masonry layout. The Extension may process images, galleries, GIFs, videos, post information, and related Reddit content in order to provide its functionality.

We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping the information processed by Mosaic limited to what is necessary to operate the Extension.

## 2. Information We Collect

Mosaic is designed to operate primarily within your browser.

### Information Processed Locally

Depending on the features you use, Mosaic may process:

- Reddit page URLs
- Subreddit names
- Reddit usernames
- Reddit post information
- Media URLs
- Image and video content
- Gallery information
- User-selected filters
- Display and layout preferences
- Playback preferences
- Extension settings

This information may be processed locally by your browser to provide Mosaic's functionality.

### Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Mosaic does not intentionally collect or store:

- Your Reddit password
- Your Reddit authentication credentials
- Private messages
- Payment card numbers
- Banking information
- Sensitive personal information
- The contents of websites unrelated to Mosaic's functionality

## 3. Reddit Content

Mosaic operates on Reddit pages that you choose to visit.

The Extension may read publicly available Reddit page content necessary to identify posts and display supported media in Mosaic's interface.

Mosaic does not claim ownership of Reddit content, images, videos, GIFs, or other media displayed through the Extension. Such content remains subject to the rights and terms of its respective owners and platforms.

## 4. Local Storage

Mosaic may use browser-provided storage mechanisms to save settings and preferences.

Examples may include:

- Masonry layout settings
- Column configuration
- Filter settings
- Playback settings
- Viewer preferences
- Launcher preferences
- Other Extension configuration

These settings are stored using browser storage provided to the Extension.

## 5. Data Transmission

Mosaic is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of user information.

The Extension does not intentionally transmit your personal information to our servers simply because you browse Reddit using Mosaic.

When Mosaic loads Reddit content or media, your browser may communicate directly with Reddit, media hosts, content delivery networks, or other third-party services required to retrieve that content.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

## 6. Analytics

The current version of Mosaic does not intentionally use third-party analytics services to create a personal profile of users.

If analytics or telemetry is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when such functionality is introduced, including information about what data is collected and why.

## 7. Advertising

The current version of Mosaic does not intentionally collect personal information for targeted advertising.

Mosaic does not sell user information to advertisers.

If advertising functionality is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the applicable data practices.

## 8. Subscriptions and Payments

Mosaic is currently provided as a free product.

A subscription-based model may be introduced in the future to provide access to unlimited views or other premium functionality.

If subscription functionality is introduced, payments may be processed by a third-party payment provider. Mosaic will not directly store complete payment card information unless explicitly stated in a future version of this Privacy Policy.

Payment providers may collect information necessary to process transactions, prevent fraud, manage subscriptions, and comply with applicable laws.

Their processing of payment information is governed by their own privacy policies.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Mosaic may interact with third-party services as necessary to provide its functionality.

These may include:

- Reddit
- Media hosting services
- Content delivery networks
- Browser-provided services
- Payment providers, if subscriptions are introduced
- Other services required to retrieve or display requested content

Mosaic does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

Users should review the privacy policies of those services when appropriate.

## 10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of information.

Because Mosaic is primarily designed to process information within the user's browser, much of the Extension's functionality can operate without sending personal information to a Mosaic-controlled server.

However, no software, browser, network connection, or online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Data Retention

Mosaic does not intentionally maintain a centralized database of users' Reddit browsing activity.

Information stored locally by the Extension remains subject to the browser's storage mechanisms and may be removed when the user:

- Uninstalls the Extension
- Clears the Extension's stored data
- Clears applicable browser data
- Resets Mosaic's settings

If future versions introduce server-side accounts or subscription services, applicable retention practices will be described in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Mosaic is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Extension.

Parents or guardians who believe that information belonging to a child has been collected may contact us using the contact information provided below.

## 13. Your Choices

You can control your use of Mosaic by:

- Installing or uninstalling the Extension
- Changing Mosaic's settings
- Clearing the Extension's stored data through your browser
- Choosing which Reddit pages to visit
- Choosing which Mosaic features to use

You may stop Mosaic from processing Reddit pages by disabling or uninstalling the Extension.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Mosaic's functionality, business model, or data practices change.

When material changes are made, the "Effective Date" at the beginning of this policy will be updated.

Continued use of Mosaic after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes use of the Extension under the updated policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
